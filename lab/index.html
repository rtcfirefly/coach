1c · Gym-noise stress no download

Plays shaped noise through the speaker while you talk, so you can test recognition and barge-in without going to the gym. Echo cancellation is what should stop this bleeding into the mic — if the VAD fires on the noise alone, AEC is not doing its job on this device.
